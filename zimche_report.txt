ZIMBABWEAN UNIVERSITIES 2018 GRADUATION STATISTICS
The Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education (ZIMCHE) was established by an Act of Parliament, Chapter 25:27, 
to register and accredit institutions of higher education. The mandate of ZIMCHE is to promote and co-ordinate 
education provided by institutions of higher education and to act as a regulator in the determination and maintenance 
of standards of teaching, examinations, academic qualifications and research in institutions of higher education. 
Every year ZIMCHE collects graduation statistics from all Zimbabwean universities. These statistics are then processed 
to produce data which is made available to stakeholders. Stakeholders can see the graduation statistics of all the 
universities in Zimbabwe from which they can make evaluations and conclusions. One can see which university 
has the highest number of graduates in a particular year and the discipline areas as well as gender distribution.
Universities themselves can compare their graduation statistics with those of the rest of the universities in the 
country which can provide some valuable lessons. From the graduation statistics institutions can also evaluate 
themselves on how much they are contributing to national socio-economic development.Below are the graduation 
statistics of the twenty-one registered Zimbabwean universities, both public and private, for the year 2018:
